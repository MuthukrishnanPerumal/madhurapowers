Professional Solar Energy Website — UI/UX Design Prompt

Create a modern, professional, simple, and highly attractive Solar Energy Company website with a premium clean-energy aesthetic.

The website should feel trustworthy, innovative, eco-friendly, modern, and conversion-focused. Avoid excessive animations, gradients, or clutter. Prioritize clear information, strong visuals, easy navigation, and excellent mobile responsiveness.

Overall Design Direction

- Modern clean-energy / renewable-energy theme
- Premium corporate appearance
- Minimal and spacious layout
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Rounded cards with subtle shadows
- High-quality solar panel and residential/commercial solar imagery
- White/off-white primary background
- Use solar yellow, warm orange, deep green, and dark navy/charcoal as accent colors
- Use a modern font such as Inter, Manrope, Poppins, or Plus Jakarta Sans
- Large headings with short, powerful messaging
- Smooth micro-interactions and subtle hover effects
- Avoid excessive green usage
- Use plenty of whitespace
- Fully responsive for desktop, tablet, and mobile

UI/UX Requirements

Make the website:

- Fully responsive
- Mobile-first
- Fast loading
- SEO-friendly
- Accessible
- Easy to navigate
- Conversion-focused

Use:

- Sticky header
- Smooth scrolling
- Subtle hover animations
- Scroll reveal animations
- Interactive cards
- Animated statistics
- Modern buttons
- Clean icons
- Proper spacing
- Consistent border radius
- Strong typography hierarchy

Avoid:

- Too many colors
- Excessive animations
- Crowded sections
- Huge blocks of text
- Cheap-looking stock imagery
- Excessive gradients
- Complicated navigation
- Unnecessary popups

Visual Style

The final website should look like a combination of:

Premium Solar Technology + Modern SaaS + Eco-Friendly Corporate Website

The overall impression should be:

Clean • Trustworthy • Premium • Sustainable • Modern • Professional

The design must immediately communicate:

“This company provides reliable solar solutions and can be trusted with a major long-term investment.”
